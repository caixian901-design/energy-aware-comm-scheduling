Description
（粘下面这段，HTML 保留换行标签）

<p>Distributed AI training is scheduled by its communication: how often the
workers synchronise gradients is the scheduler's most direct control over
makespan, and it is almost always tuned for time alone. This paper asks what
that tuning costs in energy and carbon.</p>

<p>A 512-accelerator data-parallel training pod (compute phase plus all-reduce
communication phase) is coupled to a weather-driven facility model at a
2 266 m plateau site (Xining) and to two illustrative hourly grid
carbon-intensity profiles. A three-way sweep over the synchronisation interval
k, the liquid-cooling fraction f and the carbon profile yields three results.
First, the schedule is a genuine energy decision: at f = 0.80 the fastest
schedule (k = 64) is <b>7.4 % faster</b> than the most frequent (k = 1) but
emits <b>14.1 % more carbon</b>, while the mean PUE of the two is <b>identical
to five decimal places (1.14901)</b> — the facility metric is blind to the very
decision that moves carbon by 16 %.</p>

<p>Second, the other levers are smaller or external: the whole liquid-cooling
range f = 0.60 to 1.00 changes carbon by <b>3.5 %</b>, whereas the grid
scenario changes it by <b>5.9x</b> (effective carbon intensity 123 vs
727 g/kWh) and the training start hour alone by <b>50 %</b> for a fixed
schedule. Third, the trade-off is conditional: it exists only when sparse
synchronisation costs convergence, and the crossover staleness penalty is
derived analytically as beta* = B/(A + B/k), which is <b>0.114</b> for the
parameters used here (numerically 0.116).</p>

<p>Communication scheduling should therefore be treated as an energy and
carbon objective, reported as energy-to-solution and carbon per delivered step
alongside PUE. All results are model-derived and reproduced by openly
available code with no digitised data; the limitations (parametric staleness
model, illustrative carbon profiles) are stated explicitly.</p>

5. License 说明（重要，务必看）
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

  正文、图、数据  →  CC BY 4.0
  代码            →  MIT

  本上传包两类都有。按 §2 的 License 字段处理（方案 A 分两次传，或方案 B 整包
  选 "Other (Open)" 并在 Description 里写明双授权）。

  为什么不直接选 CC BY 4.0：
    MIT 与 CC BY 4.0 都是宽松许可，本身不冲突，但如果你把 MIT 代码以
    "CC BY 4.0" 的标签发布，就改变了该代码的授权条款，作者声明的 MIT
    被覆盖。分标或选 Other 并写明，才是准确的。
